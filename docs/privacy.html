DiveTwin - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 July 2026

DiveTwin is a tool for overlaying and comparing two videos of the same dive. Your privacy is simple to describe: DiveTwin does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

Data we collect

None. DiveTwin has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. We do not receive any information about you or how you use the app.

Your videos

The videos you load stay on your device. All processing - syncing, overlaying, and exporting - happens locally. DiveTwin has no servers and makes no network connections, so your footage is never uploaded anywhere.

Photos access

DiveTwin asks for access to your photo library only so that you can (1) choose videos to compare and (2) save an exported overlay clip back to Photos, if you choose to. This access is used solely for those actions on your device.

Children

DiveTwin collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted at this URL with an updated date.
